<product_requirements>
The application, Coup de Pouce, is a community-based mobile platform for service exchange, functioning on a time-based currency (hours) rather than money.

**Core User-Facing Features:**
*   **Service Marketplace:** Users can post service ads with titles, descriptions, photos, and a price in hours. A homepage displays these ads, which can be filtered by categories (e.g., Jardinage, Bricolage).
*   **Post Lifecycle:** Service posts automatically expire and are removed after 3 days (72 hours). A real-time countdown timer () must be visible on each post, turning red when less than 2 hours remain.
*   **Exchange Flow:** A Vinted-like system where users can accept an exchange, locking the ad. The exchange is completed upon mutual confirmation (Tâche réalisée), triggering an automatic transfer of hours.
*   **User Management & Reputation:** Secure registration (with mandatory CGU acceptance via a checkbox), login, and profiles displaying ratings and exchange history.
*   **Admin Dashboard:** A private dashboard for administrators to view app statistics.
*   **Legal Compliance:** Static pages for Mentions Légales, CGU, and Politique de Confidentialité, accessible from the footer.
*   **UI/UX:** A modern, intuitive interface featuring an auto-scrolling footer menu with a glassmorphism effect, which pauses on manual interaction.
</product_requirements>
